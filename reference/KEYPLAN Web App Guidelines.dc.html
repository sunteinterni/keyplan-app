04 — STATUS AND CONTRASTKEYPLAN Web App Guidelines · Rev 1.0
Status & feedback สถานะ

CONTRAST RULES
Body text ≥ 4.5 : 1. Charcoal on Canvas is 14.6 : 1. Warm Grey on Canvas is 5.9 : 1.
Terracotta and Sage are fills. For text on Canvas use Terracotta Deep or Sage Deep.
Never colour alone. Every status has a label and a dot, so it survives black-and-white printing.
No dark surfaces. Nothing larger than a chip may be filled with Charcoal.
